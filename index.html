Google Sheet sync

Connect this device to your Logistics Desk Google Sheet. Orders, masters, the plan and the log are saved there, so your phone and PC show the same data. You can also type orders straight into the sheet's order tabs, in the same layout as PENDING ORDERS. The URL and key are saved only on this device.

Backup — everything in this tool

This covers all of it: orders on hand, party & route tags, the plan you're building, the plan log, and route library. The tool saves as you go, but a reload stays on the same device and browser it was saved on — it won't follow you to a different phone, browser, or after clearing site data. Download a backup before anything risky, and whenever you want a copy on another device.

Google Maps route

Used by the Map route button on each vehicle. Each stop is found by the party's place and pincode.

Route combinations

The corridor combos you actually send a vehicle out on — e.g. Ptta + Ktm + Idukki. Each one shows up on the Dashboard as a possible plan with its total weight, so you can see at a glance which combination has enough on hand to be worth loading.

Clear data

Clear one kind of data at a time. Each one asks you to confirm, and nothing else is touched. There's no undo, so download a backup first if you might want it back.

Upload log

Every paste you added, newest first and numbered in the order you added them. Open one to see each line and where it is now: still on hand, on a vehicle, dispatched, delivered, or deleted. Lines skipped as duplicates are listed too.

Copies in sheet layout: a date | party row, then a blank | item | qty row for each item. "Copy all shown" goes oldest upload first and follows the search and filters above. Skipped duplicates are not included.

Listed in loading order — number 1 goes into the truck first and comes out last. Place is shown here and on print for your reference; it is left out of Copy for Excel and the CSV.

Plan log

Each dispatch you log is kept here. Open one when the vehicle is back and set how much of each item stayed on the truck — a whole party, or just the odd item's balance — then apply to update Orders in one go. Nothing changes until you press Apply and confirm, and a resolved entry can be reopened to undo it.

Stock required

Every distinct item in Orders, A–Z, with qty needed across all parties. Open a row to see which parties.
